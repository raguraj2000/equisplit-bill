Design Assessment & Rationale

1. Built vs. Cut (Scoping & UX Judgment)

Built: Production SaaS dashboard design, dot matrix hex visualizations, zero-auth local state, greedy debt simplification (Min-Cash-Flow graph reduction), multi-split methods (Equal, Custom Exact, Percentage, Exclusions like "someone didn't eat"), 1-tap WhatsApp/UPI payment request formatting, member renaming, group settings editor, and custom presets.

Cut: OCR receipt scanning and multi-currency exchange rates. Why? Manual multi-currency adds clutter without solving the core pain: awkward payment reminders and complex debt loops.

2. Key Design Trade-off & Decision

Debated: Detailed Itemized Bill Checklist vs. Quick Exclusion Checkbox ("Someone didn't eat").

Decision: Chose Quick Exclusion & Percentage Split mode. Full itemized line-by-line breakdown increases cognitive friction on mobile. A quick exclusion checkbox solves 90% of real-world "I didn't order alcohol/drinks" edge cases in 2 seconds.

3. AI Tool Usage & Reflections

Prompting & Direction: Guided AI to construct a production SaaS analytics dashboard with high-contrast typography, large touch targets, zero framework bloat, and greedy debt graph reduction.

Caught & Fixed: Corrected floating-point rounding errors in multi-way split balances and ensured modal animations felt natural.

4. Next Steps with More Time

Direct Web UPI Intent links (`upi://pay?pa=...`), PWA offline caching, and QR code modal generation for instant in-person scanning.
